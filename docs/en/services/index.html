Pricing

S1 · In hospital

S1-N · Day shift in hospital

07:00-19:00

1,100,000 ₫/day

A full-time caregiver: hygiene, safe feeding (including tube feeding), repositioning to prevent sores, early mobility under medical guidance, joining ward rounds and noting advice, and a diary for the family. The family covers nights.

S1-T · Full day in hospital

24 hours

1,600,000 ₫/day

The S1-N day shift plus a night shift by a certified collaborator: staying at the bedside, scheduled repositioning, hygiene support, alerting ward nurses to anything unusual; checklist handovers at 07:00 and 19:00.

S2 · At home

S2A · 8-week home care and rehabilitation

8 weeks

9,500,000 ₫/package

16 home rehabilitation sessions with a licensed therapist; 4 nurse check-ups; a goal-based recovery plan; loan of exercise equipment.

S2B · Family coaching and follow-up

Monthly

1,500,000 ₫ + 1,800,000 ₫/month

2 home sessions where a nurse teaches the family; written materials; then follow-up: 2 home visits a month, a weekly video call, medication and check-up reminders.

S3 · Long term

S3 · Long-term care referral

When needed

Free for families

Referral to vetted partner care homes; a 2,500,000 ₫ per-client commission is paid by the care home and disclosed to the family.

Prices include VAT (if applicable). Public holidays and Tết +50%. 3-day deposit on signing, payment weekly.
