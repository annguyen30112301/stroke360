For hospitals 

A care partner with records, supervision and reporting, so bedside sitters on the ward are no longer a grey area.

Request our capability profile

What the hospital gets

  • Less load on ward nurses for basic care tasks.
  • Access control: caregivers wear name badges and uniforms, with per-shift rosters.
  • Quarterly quality reports: incidents, satisfaction, checklist compliance.
  • A free weekly class for families on the ward.

STROKE360 commits to

  • Following the orders of doctors, ward nurses and hospital rules.
  • No treatment procedures, no medication, no interference with treatment plans.
  • No marketing on the ward, no commissions to hospitals or medical staff.
  • Professional liability insurance for every caregiver.

3-month pilot

  1. Month 1

    Review

    The hospital reviews our records, processes and checklists; we agree on what is and isn't allowed.

  2. Month 2

    Pilot

    Run in one department; a supervising nurse checks at the bedside ≥ 2 times per week.

  3. Month 3

    Evaluate

    Quality report, survey of families and ward nurses; decide whether to expand.
