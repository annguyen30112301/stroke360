Counseling for patients and caregivers

After a stroke, both the patient and the family can feel sad, anxious or exhausted. You don't have to carry it alone: STROKE360 connects you with partner clinical psychologists and runs free support groups.

How are you feeling today?

Drag the slider to rate your own stress (0 = relaxed, 10 = overwhelmed). Saved only on this device, never sent anywhere.

When you need help now

If you or your relative has thoughts of self-harm: don't stay alone, call 115 or go to the nearest medical facility now.

Call 115

Counselors do not prescribe medication. When needed, they will advise you to see a specialist doctor.

Sessions

  • Caregivers

    1-on-1 counseling for family members

    Online (video or phone) · 45 min

    Say what's hard to say at home: exhaustion, guilt, worries about money and work. Find ways to keep going for the long run with a psychologist.

    At the partner psychologist's listed rate, paid directly by the family. STROKE360 charges no referral fee.

    Book a session
  • Patients

    1-on-1 counseling for patients

    At home or online · 45 min

    Mood assessment and support for sadness, anxiety and low motivation to exercise. The psychologist works with the rehab therapist and refers to a doctor when needed.

    At the partner psychologist's listed rate, paid directly by the family. STROKE360 charges no referral fee.

    Book a session
  • Whole family

    Family session

    Online or at home · 60 min

    The whole family sits down with a psychologist: share tasks fairly, talk about feelings, agree on a long-term care plan.

    At the partner psychologist's listed rate, paid directly by the family. STROKE360 charges no referral fee.

    Book a session
  • CaregiversFree

    Family support group

    Sunday evenings, 20:00-21:30 · Online · 6-10 people

    Facilitated by a psychologist, with a “companion” from a family who has been there. Listen and be heard, without judgement.
