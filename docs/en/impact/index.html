Who benefits, and how

  • Patients

    Receive technically correct care with fewer pressure sores, choking and falls; a continuous recovery path from hospital to home.

  • Families

    Keep their jobs, feel reassured by the evening diary, learn to care themselves, and are not alone.

  • Caregivers

    Around 11 million ₫/month, full insurance, legal shifts and a career path.

  • Hospitals

    Less load on nurses, control over who enters the ward, and quarterly quality reports.

  • Society

    Higher standards for the caregiving profession and wider awareness of stroke signs (BE-FAST).
